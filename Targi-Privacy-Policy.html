Targi Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Targi is a habit recovery and progress tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how your privacy is protected.

Data Collection

Targi does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers.

The app does not sell user data and does not track users across other apps or websites.

Local Storage

Targi stores your progress information locally on your device. This may include:

  • selected habit or recovery type
  • selected subtype
  • start date
  • reminder time
  • daily check-in status
  • milestones
  • money saved estimate
  • progress history

This information stays on your device and is not sent to a backend server by Targi.

Notifications

Targi may send local reminder notifications if you allow notifications on your device. These notifications are generated locally on your device and are used to remind you to check your progress.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Analytics and Tracking

Targi does not currently use analytics tools, advertising identifiers, third-party tracking, or cross-app tracking.

Data Sharing

Targi does not share your personal progress data with third parties.

Medical Disclaimer

Targi is a self-tracking and motivational tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you have severe withdrawal symptoms, urgent health concerns, or feel unsafe, contact a doctor, emergency service, or qualified professional immediately.

Children's Privacy

Targi is not designed to knowingly collect data from children. Because the app does not collect data on our servers, we do not knowingly store children's personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the updated version will be published with a new "Last updated" date.
